For physics-based games with shooting penalties, such as `Rebuilt`, we use a script named Flywheel Penalty Tagger.

<img src="/img/mason/tools/penalty_tagger.png" alt="Flywheel Penalty Tagger component" width="50%"/>

Simply add it to your robot's main flywheel/interaction roller, and set the `Game Piece Layer` to the correct one based on the penalized piece (`GamePiece1` for Rebuilt).
