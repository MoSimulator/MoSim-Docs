Due to some weird interactions with the compression force applied by the interaction rollers, we have encountered several issues with spindexers that we chose to document here.

## The Obvious Way

<img src="/img/mason/spindexer/plate_roller.png" alt="Plate roller spindexer" width="50%"/>

The obvious approach is to just use a handful of rollers to approximate the shape of your spindexer. However, we have found squat rollers such as a plate roller to be very inconsistent in behavior and nearly impossible to tune for. It also tends to cause fuel to come back out of the spindexer and is overall very difficult to get performance with.

## The Easier Way

We have found that using multiple smaller rollers that are significantly less squat allows us to get better spindexer behavior. It both looks better when fuel is moving in it and is a lot easier to get performance out of.
