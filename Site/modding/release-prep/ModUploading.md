Once you are ready to upload your mod to the Steam Workshop, ensure that you have Steam open and then open the Steam Workshop Uploader from `Tools` → `Modding` → `Steam Workshop Uploader`. Drag your modpack asset into the modpack spot in the uploader menu and then click `Create Workshop Metadata`.

## Workshop Metadata Setup

<img src="/img/mod-uploading/workshop_uploader.png" alt="Steam Workshop Uploader" width="50%"/>

The mod's title should already be filled out, however you can change this if desired. Fill out a short description for your mod if desired and add a `Preview Image`. This preview image should not be more than 1 MB and should be 16:9 aspect ratio. You can then change the visibility of the mod on the workshop and add any tags if desired (game-specific tags will be auto-applied). Once this is complete, ensure that your modpack version is set properly and then click `Build All Platforms + Stage`. Once that is complete, add a change note and click `Upload Staged Build to Steam`. This will upload your mod to the Steam Workshop, and you should be able to see your mod there. If this fails, ensure that you have Steam open and are connected to the internet. Please make a bug report on the MoSimulator [Discord server](https://discord.gg/mosim) if you face issues with uploading mods.
